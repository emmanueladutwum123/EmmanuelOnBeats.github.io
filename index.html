The story

Rhythms & strings

Emmanuel Adutwum is a Ghanaian-born, Orange County-based jazz drummer, pianist, guitarist, educator and conductor whose music blends jazz, classical, reggae, afrobeats, afrofusion, highlife and pop with the rich traditions of his Ghanaian heritage.

The rhythm began in childhood. His mother — herself a brass-band brigade drummer at the Presbyterian Church of Ghana, Abeka Congregation (now Royal Priesthood Congregation) — introduced him to the master drummer who laid his foundation. That training in Ghana's gospel tradition became the bedrock of everything that followed.

He honed his craft as a gospel drummer for congregations across Ghana — Presbyterian, Pentecost, ICGS and Family Life International Chapel — and carried those grooves to Pentecostal churches from Cleveland and Pittsburgh to California.

“Music that fosters connection — and spreads hope, love and unity.”

Over twelve years at the drum set, his artistry has widened to guitar and piano, giving him the range to move through jazz, gospel, Hillsong, highlife, afrobeat, afrofusion, reggae, pop and R&B — a cross-cultural dialogue built one groove at a time.
